## Description draft for owner review

Denver Express provides warehousing, cross-docking and freight rework in Denver. Services include pallet restacking, repalletizing, shrink wrapping, transloading and corrective handling for rejected or shifted freight. Contact the team with load details, receiver requirements and your delivery timing to confirm the appropriate service and availability. Standard hours are Monday through Friday, 8am to 4pm, with a 3:30pm receiving cutoff. After-hours and weekend receiving is by appointment. The facility is climate-controlled with no cold storage.

Keep links and promotional price claims out of the description. Validate all wording with the owner before publishing.

## Measurement and limits

Capture the prior 28 days and compare like periods: profile website clicks/call interactions where available, qualified requests by service, answered inquiries, accepted jobs, and time to first response. Do not equate a call interaction with a completed job. Use tagged official website links only after analytics and canonical handling are approved. Keep personal freight details out of analytics.

Google describes local ranking primarily in terms of relevance, distance and prominence; no payment or implementation guarantees better placement. Accurate information, current hours, photos and useful reviews are practical maintenance actions, not a promise of first position. [Google local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en), consulted 2026-09-08.

Ask for genuine, optional feedback after service without incentives or positive-review filtering. See [Google review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en) and the implementation in 07-reviews-and-case-studies.md.
