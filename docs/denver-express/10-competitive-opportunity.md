## Practical differentiation to validate

1. Driver/broker can identify the correct need without guessing which warehouse service term to use.
2. Receiver instructions and existing freight photos arrive with the request and stay with operational work.
3. An accountable person confirms suitability, scope and timing before arrival.
4. Work evidence and approved outcome records support follow-up and later case studies.

These are proposed workflow improvements. Validate them through observed calls, actual intake samples and client interviews before claiming a measurable advantage.

## Prioritization

P0: agree triage ownership and approved response expectations; retain `/rework`; implement a private, durable production intake; confirm primary contact visibility and mobile submission.

P1: connect existing supporting articles to canonical commercial pages, publish verified service details and permission-backed evidence, maintain the GBP profile and request neutral reviews consistently.

P2: use Search Console and GBP data to identify query gaps; expand content only where a distinct intent and useful evidence justify a page. Track baseline and subsequent qualified inquiries, not just raw traffic. Do not claim the 23 keywords have verified volume or promise a ranking/ROI number.
